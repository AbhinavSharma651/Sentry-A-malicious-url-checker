What Sentry looks at

Structure

Length, depth, and shape of the path and hostname — attackers favor long, nested, oddly-punctuated links.

Composition

Ratio of digits to letters, hyphen and underscore counts, raw IP addresses standing in for a domain name.

Vocabulary

Bait words like verify, secure, or account stitched into an unrelated domain.

Randomness

Character entropy — auto-generated malicious links read as noisier than a URL a person typed by hand.
